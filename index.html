Email List LTV

What an email subscriber is worth, based on the last 12 months of Vertex sales matched to the subscriber who clicked.

LTV per subscriber = revenue from the list's subscribers over the last 12 months ÷ everyone on the list. LTV per buyer = the same revenue ÷ people who bought. Covers Vertex sales from Oct 2025 (Vertex keeps 12 months). HKU counts ClickBank sales once their links carry the subscriber ID; refunds are subtracted.
